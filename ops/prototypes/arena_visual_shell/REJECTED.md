# REJECTED — do not measure against this prototype

The Owner rejected this vendored prototype. It is kept as evidence and history,
never as a reference. Nothing in the Arena is built or measured from it.

**The reference is** `ops/reference/design_arena/Chef Battles Arena v2.dc.html`.

## Why this note exists

Because the files here read exactly like a reference and nothing on them said
otherwise.

`ops/audits/arena/A06_measurement_matrix_2026-07-29.md` names this directory as
its reference source in its own lines 6–8, and every figure in that matrix was
taken from it. One of those figures — widen and flatten the octagon from aspect
1.30 to 2.05 — was handed to card A07 as the work to do. It survived six days and
was one instruction away from being built, which would have reshaped the arena
floor to a prototype the Owner had thrown away, and would have looked like the
builder's mistake rather than the matrix's.

The A06 numbers are not stated here to be wrong. Their provenance is wrong, which
is worse: a number from a rejected source might happen to be right, and nobody
can prove it either way.

## What to do with the numbers already taken from it

Treat every figure in the A06 matrix as unsourced until it is re-measured
against the Design Template. Do not build to them and do not quote them.
