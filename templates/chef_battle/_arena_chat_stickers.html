WHY A JSON MAP AND NOT A SPRITE. The eight drawings this replaces were an SVG sprite, and a sprite is right for line art a stylesheet recolours. These are finished paintings: nothing about them inherits a theme, and inlining a quarter of a megabyte of base64 into every arena page - loaded whether the picker is ever opened or not - is the opposite of what the sprite was for. WHY THE MAP IS RENDERED HERE RATHER THAN BUILT IN THE SCRIPT. Static files are served through ManifestStaticFilesStorage, so every name carries a content hash - `let_him_cook.a1b2c3d4e5f6.webp` - and only {% templatetag openblock %} static {% templatetag closeblock %} knows it. A script that assembled its own URLs would 404 on production and work perfectly in development, which is the worst shape a bug can have. The token is the key, the same token that appears in a message body as :let_him_cook:, so arena_chat.js can look one up without knowing anything about where the files live. {% endcomment %}
