Reward Queue

A Chef Battle Reward or Live Support Reward waits here until somebody releases it, and it has two possible ends that exclude each other. Issue to wallet credits the tokens to the recipient, writes the transaction and the ledger entry, and ends the record there. Approve for payout puts it on the money path instead: it pays nobody, it makes the record countable towards the {{ payout_minimum }}-token minimum for a payout the chef must still request, and that request still has to pass DAC7, the reward agreement, Stripe Connect onboarding and the compliance checks. Nothing here sets a rate or invents a grant: it releases a record the game already created. The Owner’s own account is not touched from this panel.
